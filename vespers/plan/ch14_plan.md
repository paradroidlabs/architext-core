## Thematic Throughlines — Ch14

- **Containment vs. completion:** The chapter's primary axis. Maret represents the institutional logic of containment — reasonable, responsible, and ultimately insufficient. Sable represents completion — dangerous, uncertain, and possibly the only honest response to what the signal actually is.
- **Institutional dishonesty:** Sable's performance of compliance is the book's dishonesty curve reaching a new stage. She is honest about the facts and dishonest about their implications. This is the cost of working within an institution that cannot accommodate what she knows.
- **Infrastructure as listener:** The colony's atmospheric processors are not just carrying the signal — they are *responding* to it. The built environment becomes a character. This is the "technology as prosthetic understanding" pillar rendered at architectural scale.
- **Incomplete rightness:** Maret is not wrong. This is essential. His protocol was written to protect people. His concern for Senne is genuine. His risk assessment is reasonable given what he knows. He is simply working with an incomplete picture — and the incompleteness is not his fault. Sable's institutional dishonesty is what keeps his picture incomplete.

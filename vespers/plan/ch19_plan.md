-   *Purpose:* Establish the immediate, unambiguous threat of Central Command. Show Coda's transformed community facing this threat.
-   *Key beats:*
    -   Central Command's fleet: Visually massive, a formation of black geometric hulls against the bruised twilight of Vespers. Their thermal signatures are intense, burning cold through Sable's integrated perception.
    -   First official communication from Central Command: A warning signal. Sable perceives it not just as data, but as a discordant vibration, a blunt instrument of "control." It feels like a sonic assault, but translated to haptic pressure and cold. Thresh translates its content: *Unsanctioned anomaly. Immediate compliance required. Prepare for containment protocol.*
    -   **Social texture:** The observation deck is now full of colonists. Not panicking, but standing in a communal vigil. Many hold acoustic crystals, their faces upturned towards the approaching fleet. Sable reads their collective vibration: a deep, unified hum of resolve, layered with the quiet, shared dread. They are listening to Coda's hum, drawing strength from their new "home." Sable feels their unity, a collective body bracing for impact. This is the new devotional practice applied to survival.
    -   Sable's internal register: A deep, cold resolve. No cataloguing, no filing. Only a clear understanding of the impending collision.
    -   Thresh's immediate reaction: His hand finds Sable's. His fear is present, but it's channeled into protective presence.
-   *Elaboration techniques:* Extensive haptic description of the fleet's presence and communication. Contrast the fleet's "control" vibration with Coda's "home" hum. Sable's internal processing as a channel for collective emotion. The visual of the fleet, the haptic of its intent, the emotional resonance of the colonists. (~1500-2000 words)

**Scene 2 — The Council of Coda**

-   *Entry mechanic:* TIME JUMP. After the initial contact. Sable, Thresh, Maret, Prael, and perhaps a few senior technicians/colony representatives are gathered in Maret's (now less formal) office.
-   *Purpose:* Forge Coda's unified defense strategy. Cement Maret's and Prael's alignment. Sable proposes "the Answer" as the only viable defense.
-   *Key beats:*
    -   Maret's new posture: No longer administrative. He stands with Sable, indicating his full commitment to Coda's defense against Central Command. He outlines Central Command's standard operating procedure: total suppression, followed by erasure of any affected infrastructure. He presents this not as a warning, but as the inevitable outcome if Coda does not act.
    -   Prael's intervention: They speak (through Thresh's translation) with clinical precision. Their observation of Senne's "translation completed" state in Ch18 is key. They state that Coda's current harmonic signature represents an "unprecedented stability" for a Stage 5 Resonance event. They recommend *non-interference* with Coda's internal state, a direct contradiction to Central Command's protocol. This is the Archive's deeper, philosophical mandate asserting itself. They implicitly support Sable's solution.
    -   Sable's proposal: She signs clearly, directly (Thresh translating for all). The signal is not a threat; it is a conversation. Central Command seeks to impose silence ("control"). Coda must respond with completion ("home"). Her dive to the core is not just to close the prior universe's channel, but to *broadcast* Coda's new, stable reality to Central Command. The completed signal, she argues, will be a deterrent because it offers a state beyond their comprehension or control. It is a new baseline.
    -   Debate: Brief, focused questions from colony reps about the risk, the timeline. Sable addresses them with calm conviction, detailing the coordinates, the integration of the phantom, the photograph's message.
    -   Decision: Maret gives the final, executive order: Sable has full access to the *Meridian* and all necessary resources. Prael offers technical support for the dive systems, especially the modified transducers. The council agrees.
-   *Elaboration techniques:* Complex dialogue, intricate haptic readings of Maret's and Prael's non-verbal communication (posture, hand gestures, shifts in breathing for Maret; Prael's methodical, precise hand movements). Sable's conviction must be palpable through her focused signing. The scene builds tension through strategic discussion. (~3000-3500 words)

She is *broadcasting* the entire, integrated experience of Coda, the hum, the feeling of "home," the knowledge of completion. She is becoming the Answer. The sensation of a vast, ancient entity finally sighing, exhaling. The conversation closes. Not destroyed. *Retreated.* Their probes are gone. Their containment protocol has ceased. They received the Answer. And they chose not to engage. The silence of their departure is profound.
    -   Coda's state: Sable feels the colony through the floor. It is still. It is home. The shared dread from Scene 1 has been replaced by a quiet, collective sense of awe and profound peace. The Answer has stabilized it.

#### Action 4.4: Granular Scene Planning

**Scene 1 — The Quiet**

*   **Entry mechanic:** HARD CUT. Opens on Sable and Thresh already in Senne's medical unit.
*   **Purpose:** Resolve Senne's character arc. Confirm her "translation completed" state. Provide emotional closure for Thresh and Sable.
*   **Key beats:**
    *   Senne is lying in her bed. Her vital signs are stable, but her body is profoundly still. Not the stillness of sleep, but of something finished. Her tremor is gone. Her breath is regular, shallow. Her face is calm, a faint smile.
    *   Sable approaches. Places her integrated right hand (phantom warmth now part of her) on Senne's forehead. Reads Senne's internal vibration: it is coherent. It carries the new harmonic of Coda, but in a purified, distilled form. It is the silence of a completed conversation, held within a human frame. Senne is not gone. She is *translated*. Her consciousness has completed its integration with the prior universe's signal.
    *   Thresh is beside Sable. He doesn't touch Senne. He watches Sable's face, reading *her* for what he cannot perceive in his sister. Sable turns to him, signs: *She is home.* Not a lie. Not a euphemism. The truth.
    *   Thresh's reaction: A single, visible tear. Not a sob. A quiet acceptance. He places his hand over Sable's on Senne's forehead. He cannot feel the harmonic, but he accepts Sable's reading. He signs: *Then so are we.* (This is his acceptance of the new Coda, the new reality, and Sable's role within it.)
    *   A Prael technician enters, observes Senne's state, and notes the "unprecedented stability post-Stage 4 manifestation." They do not attempt intervention. They document.
    *   Sable and Thresh leave the unit, leaving Senne in this quiet state.
*   **Elaboration techniques:** Focus on Sable's new, unmediated perception of Senne's internal state. Haptic description of Senne's stillness and internal harmonic. Thresh's non-verbal communication and internal emotional processing. Prael's clinical observation contrasts with the emotional intimacy.
*   **Depth justification:** This scene provides crucial emotional closure for a major character, leveraging Sable's transformed perception to give meaning to Senne's fate beyond simple loss. It grounds the cosmic event in personal grief and acceptance.

**Scene 2 — The Corridor's Hum** *(Social Texture Beat)*

*   **Entry mechanic:** TIME JUMP. Sable and Thresh are walking through a transformed residential corridor, hours later.
*   **Purpose:** Explore Coda's new harmonic through Sable's perspective. Deliver the social texture beat, showing how colonists are reacting.
*   **Key beats:**
    *   The residential corridor: it is alive with the new harmonic. Sable perceives it everywhere — through her feet on the floor, the walls she passes, the air that stirs. It is not oppressive. It is *present*. It feels like the integrated phantom warmth, expanded to colony scale. It is the sound of "home."
    *   She passes a group of colonists sitting on benches. They are not talking. They are holding small acoustic crystals (like the one above the maintenance tech's door from Ch6, or the market crystals from Ch10) to their chest or temples. They are quietly *listening* to the new harmonic. Their expressions are varied: awe, quiet wonder, peace, some fear, but mostly profound attentiveness. This is Coda's new devotional practice.
    *   Sable and Thresh walk past the maintenance tech's door from Ch6. The hand-made bracket is still there, but the fossil is gone. In its place, a small, intricate drawing etched into the composite wall — a geometric pattern identical to Sable's first notation for the signal, but simplified, communal. The tech has moved from individual devotion to communal expression of the new reality.
    *   Thresh feels the harmonic only as a diffuse, low-frequency hum through his feet. He cannot parse its complexity. He holds Sable's hand. He trusts her reading. He signs: *They are listening.* Sable signs: *They are home.*
    *   The corridor's ambient temperature is consistently 0.5°C warmer than pre-Ch17 baseline, felt by Sable as a pervasive, gentle warmth. This is Coda's new material reality.
*   **Elaboration techniques:** Extensive haptic description of the new harmonic through Sable's integrated perception. Contrast with Thresh's limited perception. Visual details of colonists' devotional practice. The etched drawing provides a visual callback to Sable's notation.
*   **Depth justification:** This scene establishes the immediate, visible consequences of the signal's completion on the colony's social and physical environment, fulfilling the social texture mandate and Sable's new role as an interpreter of this reality.

**Scene 3 — The Correspondent**

*   **Entry mechanic:** IN-MEDIAS-RES. Sable is already in her quarters. The photograph is already in her hand.
*   **Purpose:** Resolve the photograph thread. Identify the face as the correspondent. Connect it to the completed signal.
*   **Key beats:**
    *   Sable is holding the photograph, face-up. The face of the unknown woman is there, mouth open, expression of urgency. Since Ch10, Sable has known the mouth's shape corresponds to Symbol 1 of her notation.
    *   Now, with her unmediated understanding, she looks at the face. She feels the last transmission, the completed word: "home." The shape of the woman's mouth in the photograph is not just Symbol 1. It is the precise haptic geometry of the word "home," as she felt it in the core. The urgency is not terror. It is the urgency of *speaking* that word, of reaching "home" through her transmission.
    *   The face is the sender. The correspondent. Not an abstract entity, but a specific consciousness who, in its last moments, reached out across universes with the word "home."
    *   Sable places her integrated right hand over the face in the photograph. The phantom warmth, now fully integrated, resonates with the image. It is not her mother. It was never her mother. It was this being, whose last impression of impossible love and home resonated with Sable's own deepest, unacknowledged need. Sable feels a profound connection, not of loss, but of *shared ending*.
    *   She keeps the photograph face-up on her desk. It is no longer a mystery or a wound. It is an address.
    *   Thresh is not present. This is Sable's solitary, intimate discovery.
*   **Elaboration techniques:** Deep internal monologue as Sable processes the visual and haptic information. Detailed haptic translation of the mouth's shape as the word "home." The emotional weight comes from the shift from perceived terror to understood urgency, and from the familial phantom to cosmic connection.
*   **Depth justification:** This scene provides the ultimate resolution for a key symbol, tying together Sable's personal history, the cosmic signal, and the core themes of identity, memory, and home. It solidifies Sable's new understanding and role.

**Scene 4 — The Horizon**

*   **Entry mechanic:** HARD CUT. Sable and Thresh are already on the *Meridian*'s observation deck.
*   **Purpose:** Establish Central Command's arrival or imminent arrival. Concrete external threat. Reframe the stakes.
*   **Key beats:**
    *   Sable and Thresh stand on the *Meridian*'s observation deck, looking out over Vespers' bruised-purple twilight.
    *   On the horizon, a new light appears. Not a storm, not a Coda vessel. A cluster of distinct, high-intensity thermal signatures, moving with deliberate velocity. A fleet. Central Command.
    *   Sable's integrated perception reads the fleet's vibration through the *Meridian*'s hull and the ambient atmosphere: these are not Archive security ships. These are heavily armed, long-range vessels. Their intent is not observation or containment. It is *control*. They perceive Coda as an anomaly to be suppressed, not a transformed world.
    *   Thresh reads Sable's face, her micro-expressions. He signs: *They are here for the silence.*
    *   Sable signs: *They are here for what we became.* She places her hand on the *Meridian*'s hull. The ship, which repaired itself, now hums with the new harmonic. It is ready.
    *   The chapter ends with the fleet's approach, a new, external tension rising against the internal peace achieved.
*   **Elaboration techniques:** Focus on the visual description of the approaching fleet. Sable's haptic reading of their intent, contrasting with Coda's new harmonic. Thresh's succinct, perceptive dialogue.
*   **Depth justification:** This scene reintroduces the external conflict, shifting the narrative focus from internal resolution to the defense of Coda's transformed state. It sets up the next act with a clear, high-stakes confrontation.

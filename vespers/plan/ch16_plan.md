### Action 4.4: Granular Scene Planning

**Scene 1 — The Airlock**

*   *Entry mechanic:* IN-MEDIAS-RES. Opens with Sable already in her dive suit, at the *Meridian*'s airlock, hands on the inner hatch, Thresh making final external checks.
*   *Purpose:* Finalize preparations, establish Sable's understanding of the terminal nature of the dive, and acknowledge the physiological reality of the metallic hydrogen core.
*   *Key beats:*
    *   Sable's hands on the airlock hatch, feeling the subtle vibration of the station. Thresh's final external checks, visible through the viewport. His movements are precise, almost ritualistic.
    *   **Sable's Internal Calculus of Death:** Her internal monologue details the theoretical physics of metallic hydrogen at extreme depth. Not fear, but data. She reviews the *Meridian*'s armored hull, not as a means of survival, but as a buffer to buy seconds before structural collapse, to complete the transmission. She visualizes the hull crumpling, the transducers ceasing. This is a one-way trip, a calculated sacrifice.
    *   Thresh signals the all-clear for pressurization. His eyes meet hers through the thick glass. No words, just a shared moment of grim understanding.
*   *Elaboration techniques:* Deep sensory immersion in the airlock environment – the cold metal, the hum of systems, Thresh’s precise movements. Sable’s internal monologue is clinical, but the context makes it profoundly emotional. Focus on the contrast between the mundane actions and the extreme stakes.

**Scene 2 — The Bridge** *(Social Texture Beat)*

*   *Entry mechanic:* HARD CUT. Scene opens immediately after pressurization, *Meridian* is now sealed, beginning its pre-descent checks, but still docked. Thresh is inside the cabin with Sable.
*   *Purpose:* Reveal Thresh's independent work, explicitly link Senne's immediate decline to the dive's urgency, and introduce the organized devotional response on Coda.
*   *Key beats:*
    *   Thresh is at his console, but he's not monitoring standard pre-dive. He hands Sable a small, folded piece of paper. Not a printed report, but a hand-written note in their cabin-specific sign language.
    *   **Thresh's Independent Work:** The note is a transcription of Senne's last coherent transmissions. Not words, but patterns of haptic tremor and neural oscillation data he's been secretly logging. It contains a specific, repeating pattern Sable recognizes as a fragmented part of Coordinate 10, rendered through Senne's compromised system. He has been acting as a translator for his sister's dying moments.
    *   **Senne's Imminence:** Thresh signs (Sable reading his hands directly, not through transducers): *"She won't last another 48 hours. This is all she has left."* This clarifies that the dive cannot wait for further lab-based coordinate derivation. They must dive *now*.
    *   **Social Texture (Organized Devotional Response):** As Thresh signs, Sable feels a distant, rhythmic vibration through the *Meridian*'s hull, transmitted from the colony's central structure. It’s an irregular pulse, not mechanical, but collective. Thresh glances at a small, unofficial console display he’s rigged – showing a map of Coda with several nodes pulsing in sync with the vibration. He signs: *"The stations. They're making a record."* The colonists are collectively attempting to "record" or "bear witness" to the escalating Resonance, an organized, haptic devotional practice in response to Stage 4. This is the first public, organized expression of Coda's acoustic theology.
    *   Sable understands: Thresh is not just her partner; he is Senne's bridge, and now a witness to the colony's collective grief. He has used his own skills, outside the Archive's protocol, to gather this.
*   *Elaboration techniques:* The handwritten note as a physical artifact of Thresh's defiance. The emotional weight of Thresh's direct communication about Senne. The description of the collective vibration as a structured, rhythmic pulse, interpreted through the *Meridian*'s hull. The unofficial console display adds a visual layer to the social texture.

**Scene 3 — The Descent Continues**

*   *Entry mechanic:* TIME JUMP. Scene opens with the *Meridian* already deep in the atmosphere, past the cloud layer, having begun its descent toward the metallic hydrogen core. The initial departure is elided.
*   *Purpose:* Commence the full descent, emphasize the extreme environment, and dramatize the derivation of the remaining coordinates (10, 11, 12) under immense pressure.
*   *Key beats:*
    *   The *Meridian* descends through crushing pressure. The hull groans, a deep, resonant tremor felt through Sable's spine. Colors outside the viewport shift from bruised purples to deepening, pressure-rendered blacks, then to an impossible metallic sheen as they approach the hydrogen core.
    *   Sable, using Thresh's partial data for Coordinate 10 and her own attuned nodes, attempts to derive the remaining coordinates. This is done through a combination of haptic emission (using the transducers to "probe" the ambient carrier frequency for a response) and analysis of the subtle shifts in the hull's vibration as they pass through different atmospheric layers.
    *   **Coordinate 10 Derivation:** The first attempt yields a violent feedback loop – a sharp, burning geometry across her nodes. Thresh quickly recalibrates, reducing output. Sable persists, refining the emission. She eventually receives a fragmented, but identifiable, response – Coordinate 10: *VELOCITY (TRANSDUCED)*. It's a measure of speed, but felt as a pressure differential.
    *   **Coordinate 11 Derivation:** Further descent. The pressure intensifies. The hull composite begins to weep moisture – not a leak, but a stress reaction. Sable's nodes hum with increased warmth. Coordinate 11: *MASS (STRUCTURAL)*. A measure of density and weight, felt as bone-deep resonance. Derived from the hull's stress patterns.
    *   **Coordinate 12 Derivation:** The *Meridian* pushes deeper. Warnings flash on Thresh's console: structural integrity approaching limits. Sable knows they have only minutes. She pushes past the pain from her transducers, past the internal warnings. Coordinate 12: *GRAVITY (RELATIONAL)*. A measure of attractive force, felt as a deep, pulling sensation, not just from the planet, but from the signal itself, as if drawing her in.
    *   All 12 coordinates derived. The *Meridian* is at its structural limits. They are at the threshold of metallic hydrogen.
*   *Elaboration techniques:* Intense haptic description of pressure, heat, and structural stress. The coordinate derivation is a struggle against both the environment and hardware limits. The progression of coordinates from velocity to mass to gravity should feel increasingly fundamental and abstract.

**Scene 4 — The Signal**

*   *Entry mechanic:* HARD CUT. Scene opens with the *Meridian* holding steady at its maximum rated crush depth, just above the metallic hydrogen core. The atmosphere is an impossible metallic sheen outside.
*   *Purpose:* Acknowledge Prael's status (or lack thereof), Sable feels the direct presence of the signal, and they prepare for the final transmission.
*   *Key beats:*
    *   The *Meridian* is barely holding. Every structural member sings a high-frequency stress note through Sable's spine. Thresh is monitoring everything, his face grim but focused.
    *   Sable thinks of the Archive. Of Prael. She feels nothing from the colony now, only the overwhelming presence of Vespers, and the signal pulsing through it. Prael's absence is not a mystery; it is a consequence of Maret's action. The Archive, now aware of Maret's defection and the central command's impending arrival, has likely gone into full lockdown, Prael caught within its strictures. The institutional thread is severed, at least for now.
    *   Through her fully attuned nodes, Sable feels the signal directly. Not just the carrier frequency, but the underlying complexity of the original transmission. It is not geometric anymore. It is a presence, a vast, patient consciousness. She feels the phantom warmth, concentrated, directed, *waiting*.
    *   Thresh looks at her. Signs: *"Ready?"*
    *   Sable nods. She has all 12 coordinates. The signal is here. She is here.
*   *Elaboration techniques:* Sustained sensory description of the *Meridian* at its absolute limit. The metallic sheen of the atmosphere outside. Sable's internal processing of Prael's absence – not as a plot hole, but as an understood consequence of the institutional collapse. The direct, overwhelming presence of the signal through her nodes, culminating in the phantom warmth. The final, stark exchange between Sable and Thresh.

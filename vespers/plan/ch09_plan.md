### Action 4.2 — Chapter Goal, Arc & Working Title

**Primary purpose:** Sable develops the notation system from 8 failed geometry sheets into twelve working symbols — a haptic transcription language that can represent the three impression classes (geometry, warmth, recognition) and their combinations. This is the chapter where her relationship to the signal becomes *linguistic*: she is building a vocabulary for reception that the Archive does not have and cannot classify. The twelve symbols are the novel's first human language for a voice from a dead universe.

**Secondary purpose:** Surface Sable's external motivation. She came to Coda on a three-season contract with a harvest quota. Quota fulfillment earns a transfer recommendation to a permanent core-facility posting — the material reward for institutional compliance. The quota has been invisible because the signal consumed her attention. It surfaces here because the notation work forces a confrontation: she is spending her time building a transcription system for an unclassified signal instead of diving for harvestable fossils. She is jeopardizing her contract. The tension between what the quota represents (institutional advancement through extraction) and what the notation represents (personal language through reception) is the chapter's P5 engine: conquest vs. completion made material.

**Tertiary purpose:** Establish the convergence — the signal is teaching Sable through direct transducer contact and teaching Senne through Stage 3 biological inheritance, and both paths are producing the same language. The notation's final four symbols (recognition class) arrive not from the column but from the discovery that Senne's inherited marks are *cognate* with Sable's direct-contact symbols. This collapses the distance between protagonist and compromised subject: they are both receiving the same transmission, through different channels, and arriving at the same grammar.

**Working title:** *Notation* — the act of making marks for something that resists marking. Parallel single-word structure to Ch6 (*Baseline*), Ch7 (*Descent*). The word carries the double meaning of musical notation (a system for encoding performance) and mathematical notation (a system for encoding relationships). Both apply: the twelve symbols encode something that is simultaneously expressive (a voice) and structural (a pressure architecture).

**Specific plot points to hit:**

1. Sable develops the first eight symbols (four geometry, four warmth) through extended working sequences at her desk and at the wall breach
2. The wall breach has expanded — approximately 40cm growth in each direction since Ch8, plus a second smaller anomalous section ~30m further along the outer ring corridor
3. Quota notification surfaces Sable's external motivation: she is 14 fossils below contractual minimum with 6 weeks remaining
4. Thresh brings Sable information about Senne's marks — drawings Senne made unprompted during medical observation that are cognate with Sable's notation
5. The convergence produces the final four symbols (recognition class) — the marks both women would make
6. Social texture: educational — children in the residential ring being taught about acoustic fossils (institutional worship reproduced generationally)

**Character arc (Sable):** From informed witness (Ch8 close) to linguistic constructor. The Hearth disclosure gave her the cost. The notation gives her the *tool*. She is building something no one at Hearth built (as far as anyone knows): a language for receiving the signal rather than classifying, containing, or destroying it. The quota notification forces her to acknowledge what she is sacrificing — institutional advancement, material stability — for something she cannot explain to the institution. The Senne convergence transforms the notation from *her* personal project into something larger: the signal is building its own reception language through every available channel, and Sable happens to be the one writing it down.

**Character arc (Thresh):** His independence manifests as action — he brings Sable information the institution would not share (Senne's marks from medical observation). He is no longer the relay between Sable and Maret. He is the relay between Sable and Senne. The question he asks — *Can you help her?* — is the first time he has asked Sable for something on his own behalf, not on the Archive's.

### Action 4.4 — Granular Scene Planning

**Scene 1 — The Pressure**

- *Entry mechanic:* IN-MEDIAS-RES. Opens with Sable's pen on paper, mid-mark. The chapter begins in the physical act of notation. No preamble.
- *Purpose:* The representational breakthrough. First four symbols (geometry class). Social texture beat. Establish the chapter's register: *linguistic construction* — building marks, not filing data.
- *Key beats:*
    - The desk: notation papers spread, the 8 failed geometry sheets from Ch7 pinned to the wall above the desk (she has kept all of them — they are the problem stated visually). Each sheet tried to represent the room's *shape* — ceiling height, wall angles, console contour. All failed because they imposed a visual/spatial grammar on a haptic/pressure experience.
    - The breakthrough: she is re-reading the Ch7 sheets and realizes the distortion. Her transducers do not receive geometry as *shape*. They receive it as *pressure differentials* — the ceiling is not "4.2 meters high" to her fingertips, it is a specific pressure gradient that changes character at the boundary between open space above and solid structure below. The walls are not "amber" or "vertical" — they are pressure interfaces where the room's internal atmosphere meets a solid boundary, each wall producing a slightly different thermal/pressure signature depending on its material composition and its relationship to the room's convective patterns. The console is not a shape — it is a concavity that redirects pressure toward the seven depressions, each depression a pressure minimum that her transducer nodes read as an invitation: *place your spine here*.
    - The insight unlocks the first four symbols. She does not draw them as shapes. She draws them as *pressure relationships* — marks that represent what happens between a surface and a transducer when they meet. Each symbol is a different relationship: (1) the gradient of open space terminating at a boundary (ceiling/floor), (2) the lateral pressure of a vertical surface with specific thermal conductivity (walls), (3) the focused concavity that directs pressure toward a minimum (the console), (4) the repeating minimum pattern — seven depressions at 2–3–2–3–2–3 cm intervals, the console's keyboard, the novel's recurring structural motif.
    - She writes the four symbols in sequence on a clean sheet. They look like nothing in the Archive's notation system. They do not resemble acoustic frequency maps or geological cross-sections or any institutional notation she has been trained to use. They are *hers* — a personal language emerging from the space between her transducers and the signal's architecture.
    - **Social texture beat (recalled):** Earlier that morning, returning from the mess hall through the residential ring. She passed a space she had read before as "multi-occupancy activity room" — a generic institutional designation for a space where people gathered. Today she read it more carefully. Through the floor: a cluster of small weight patterns (children, 8–12 based on mass distribution and gait frequency), one larger weight pattern (adult, standing, shifting weight periodically), and the specific micro-vibration of a crystalline specimen being passed from hand to hand — the telltale oscillation of an acoustic fossil's internal crystal structure excited by body heat and handling pressure. Someone was teaching children about fossils. The institutional relationship to signal — acoustic paleontology as *subject*, fossils as *lesson material*, the Archive's taxonomy as *curriculum* — reproduced at the generational level. The children were learning to read fossils the way Sable was taught: as artifacts, as data, as preserved signal to be classified and catalogued. And in her quarters, she was building marks for the signal as *presence*. Two educational projects, running in parallel, producing languages for two different relationships to the same phenomenon.
- *Elaboration techniques:* The breakthrough from spatial to pressure representation must be rendered through Sable's internal reasoning — the reader follows her logic step by step, feels the "click" of the insight. Each of the four symbols should be described physically (what the mark looks like on paper) and conceptually (what pressure relationship it encodes). The social texture beat (teaching space) is recalled, not experienced in real-time — interwoven with the desk work as contrast.

Each one requires approximately 6–8 hours of dive time — descent, survey, identification, harvest, ascent, processing. At her current rate (which has been zero harvestable dives since the recalibration), she would need to schedule approximately 2–3 harvest dives per week for the remaining season. This is achievable. This is what she was doing before the signal consumed her attention.
    - Her contract: three seasons at Coda, standard quota, in exchange for a transfer recommendation to a permanent posting at a core Archive facility. The posting is not abstract — it represents stability, institutional standing, access to the Archive's deep collection (fossils she has only read about in classification indices). She came to Coda *for this*. Three seasons in a deep-atmospheric settlement, doing institutional work at institutional pace, earning institutional reward. The contract is the reason she is here. The signal is the reason she stayed.
    - She stares at the notification and understands what she has done: she has spent three weeks building a personal language for an unclassified signal while her contractual obligation — the thing that will determine her professional future — accrues a deficit she cannot explain to the institution. She cannot tell the Archive that her dives have been communicative. She cannot explain the notation. She cannot file the wall readings. Everything she has done since the recalibration exists in a parallel information architecture that the institution cannot see and would not understand.
    - The philosophical weight: the quota represents *extraction* — harvesting fossils from the deep atmosphere and delivering them to the Archive for classification and preservation. The notation represents *reception* — building a language to receive a signal that the Archive's classification system categorizes as hazard. The two activities are structurally identical (both involve a diver, a transducer, a signal, a record) and philosophically opposed (one takes, the other receives). Sable cannot do both. Every hour spent on the notation is an hour not spent on the quota, and the quota is the institution's only measure of her value.
    - She does not respond to the notification. She closes the terminal. She returns to the desk.
    - But the number stays. 14. The external motivation is not dismissed — it is *held*, the way the Hearth disclosure is held, the way the wall boundary is held. Another piece of evidence that the baseline — her professional baseline, her contractual baseline, her relationship to the institution that employs her — is no longer reliable.
- *Elaboration techniques:* The quota notification rendered in full institutional language — the contrast between the admin system's automated precision and the notation sheets on the desk is the scene's visual engine. Sable's internal reasoning about the contract should be specific (what the posting means, why she wants it, what it costs to lose it) without becoming backstory dump — fold it into her present-tense processing. The philosophical weight (extraction vs. reception) should emerge from the juxtaposition, not from stated theme.
- *Estimated word count:* ~1,500

Thresh saw the flag in the shared medical log (he has maintained access to Senne's file as her listed next-of-kin) and photographed the form before the medical officer could file it into the institutional system where it would be categorized as pathology.
    - The marks: not identical to Sable's notation. Different hand, different pressure, different instrument (Senne used the observation form's pen, standard issue, not Sable's preferred technical drafting pen). But the pressure relationships are *cognate*. Sable sees it immediately — the way a linguist sees cognate words in two related languages: the roots are the same, the surface forms differ. Senne's marks describe the same pressure architecture Sable has been notating: the gradient, the lateral surface, the concavity. The geometry class. Not learned from the column — Senne has never been to the column, never worn a dive suit, never touched the crystallized shockwave formation at 547 atm. They are in her body now, the way fossil coordinates are in her body, the way the wall boundary is in her body.
    - He leaves. She is alone with the twelve symbols.
    - **Chapter close:** Sable picks up the sheet with all twelve marks and holds it in front of her. Four for the room's architecture. Four for the warmth that knows she is there. Four for the presence that has been waiting a billion years.     - She does not file the twelve symbols. She does not encrypt them. She does not hide them. She leaves them on the desk, face-up, in the light. The first notation that does not need to be concealed because it is written in a language no one else can read.

### Action 4.6 — Pacing & Flow Plan

Ch9 is a *construction* chapter — the pacing mirrors the act of building a language. Scene 1 is the breakthrough (the "click" of insight, the first four symbols arriving from a reframed understanding). Scene 2 is field testing (taking the new language to the evidence, discovering both that it works and that the evidence has changed). Scene 3 is confrontation (the institutional world intruding on the private work, the cost made visible). Scene 4 is convergence (the private language discovered to be shared, the final symbols arriving from an unexpected source).

**Emotional shape:** breakthrough → verification/escalation → confrontation → convergence.

Ch8 was the novel's heaviest information-delivery chapter (Hearth disclosure). Ch9 is deliberately different: it is a *making* chapter, not a *receiving* chapter. Sable is active — building, testing, writing, comparing. The energy is constructive rather than testimonial. This prevents the "post-disclosure valley" flagged in the Alignment Dock.

The chapter's intellectual demands (describing twelve symbols and their conceptual basis) are distributed across Scenes 1, 2, and 4: four symbols each. This prevents any single scene from becoming a symbol catalogue. Each group of four arrives in a different context (desk, wall, convergence) and through a different process (insight, field observation, comparative reading).

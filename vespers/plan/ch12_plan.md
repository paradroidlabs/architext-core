### Action 4.2 — Chapter Goal, Arc & Working Title

**Primary purpose:** Sable begins building the instrument she needs to answer the transmission. The transducer array was designed to *receive* — to capture acoustic signatures, to read pressure, to convert atmospheric data into neurological input. Answering the signal requires reversing this function: using the same hardware to *emit*. This is the chapter's central technical and philosophical challenge — turning a listening device into a speaking device. The hardware reversal is both literal (reconfiguring transducer nodes for emission) and metaphorical (Sable reversing from observer to actor, from cataloguer to correspondent).

**Secondary purpose:** Reactivate the dormant voice content thread. During the first hardware reversal test — running the transducer array in emission mode with the notation's coordinates loaded — Sable encounters a fragment of the original voice. Not the full sentence. Not even words she can parse. But the quality of *speech* — the rhythmic, intentional, communicative structure of a voice that was saying something when it was captured. The fragment is carried in the fourth coordinate (the first new one she reverse-engineers from the notation grammar in this chapter), confirming that the twelve coordinates are not abstract mathematical objects but *components of a voice* — the signal is a sentence, and the notation is its alphabet. This reframes the entire transmission: Sable is not answering a signal. She is completing someone else's sentence.

**Tertiary purpose:** Introduce the fabrication workshop as a new social texture space and bring 1–2 named colonists into the narrative. Sable needs hardware modifications she cannot perform alone — the transducer array's emission reversal requires physical reconfiguration of the fingertip nodes, work that needs a fabrication specialist's tools and expertise. This brings her into the colony's technical infrastructure for the first time as a *collaborator* rather than an institutional observer.

**Working title:** *The Instrument* — triple meaning. The transducer array as instrument (tool). Sable as instrument (the signal's intended receiver/transmitter). The chapter itself as the instrument of narrative transition from receiving to transmitting. Also carries the musical connotation: an instrument is something that produces sound, not just captures it.

**Internal processing register:** *Construction/commitment* — Sable is building. The fear from Ch11 is present but carried, not paralyzing. The register should feel like someone at a workbench: focused, methodical, problem-solving. Not the institutional methodology of the cataloguing framework (which is broken), but a *personal* methodology — the approach of someone who is doing this because it needs doing, not because the institution requires it. The growth register: post-sentence, post-threshold, pre-dive.

# Phase 6: Continuity & Quality Analysis — Chapter 14

### 1. What Works
- **The Dampening Field as Haptic Absence:** The description of the localized purge field as an "acoustic black hole" effectively leans into the haptic POV rules. It translates abstract sci-fi tech into a deeply sensory, physical horror for a protagonist whose entire worldview is based on vibration and texture.
- **The Ideological Confrontation:** Maret is not written as a mustache-twirling villain. His motivation (sacrificing Coda to save the wider Archive, citing the Hearth precedent) makes him a rational institutionalist facing an impossible anomaly.
- **Senne's Vocalization:** The IN-MEDIAS-RES entry into Senne speaking Maret's prior-universe name delivers excellent emotional shock. The transition from abstract institutional threat (the purge field) to raw biological confrontation (Senne) works perfectly to break Maret's resolve.
- **Deriving Coordinate 8:** The integration of the coordinate derivation (CAUSAL WEIGHT) directly into the emotional climax of the scene is elegant. It turns the narrative climax into a puzzle-solving step simultaneously.

### 2. What is Unclear/Missing
- **Sable's Lack of Haptic Response to Maret's Fear:** While we see Maret stagger back, the text misses an opportunity to describe Maret's physiological terror through Sable's haptic reading (e.g., feeling his accelerated heartbeat spike through the deck plates). It's described visually ("His hand came up to his mouth") rather than through the specialized sensory lens.
- **The Mechanics of the Cancelation:** In the final scene, Maret cancels the deployment order with a touch to his terminal. It happens too cleanly. The bureaucratic weight of cancelling an Archive-level purge protocol should require more friction or consequence. He just types a command and the threat is over.
- **Prael's Absence:** Prael, who triggered this entire institutional crackdown in Ch13, is completely unmentioned in the fallout. Since they conducted the formal review, would they not be present or notified of the protocol's cancellation?

### 3. Recommended Edits
- **Revise Scene 3 (The Demonstration) to enhance haptic tracking:** Add two sentences detailing Sable reading Maret's physiological collapse through the floorboards—the spike in his heart rate, the sudden irregularity in his breathing, translating his emotional shock into raw data.
- **Revise Scene 4 (The Silence) to add friction to the cancellation:** Have Maret verbalize the cost of his action. For example, "When central command sees the cancellation log, they will send a containment squad. We have perhaps two weeks before they arrive." This introduces a new institutional clock to replace the immediate threat of the purge field.
- **Acknowledge Prael in Scene 4:** Add a beat where Maret notes that Prael's report has already been filed, ensuring the overarching Archive threat remains active even if the immediate purge is cancelled.

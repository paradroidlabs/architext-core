# Phase 6: Continuity & Quality Analysis — Chapter 15

### 1. What Works
- **The Shift in Tone:** The transition from institutional dread in Ch14 to the heavy, mechanical inevitability of the deep dive preparation works extremely well. The focus on stripping mass to armor the *Meridian* grounds the upcoming climax in physical, industrial reality.
- **Maret's Complicity:** Having Maret deliver the restricted armor without speaking is powerful. He is funding the suicide mission he opposed, recognizing that his purge protocol is broken and Sable's completion protocol is the only alternative.
- **Coordinate 9 Derivation:** Pulling Coordinate 9 from the acoustic reaction of the plasma torch against the Archive metal is a perfect integration of sci-fi engineering and the combinatorial grammar system. It avoids repetitive emission tests by making the act of building the ship the instrument itself.
- **Thresh's Goodbye:** The final scene with Senne is appropriately muted. It avoids melodrama. His grief is communicated haptically through Sable's reading of his heartbeat and micro-tremors, keeping the POV constraint intact.

### 2. What is Unclear/Missing
- **Sable's Internal Calculus of Death:** Sable knows this is a one-way trip. The *Meridian* is being armored, but it is descending into metallic hydrogen. The text needs a beat where Sable explicitly catalogs the physiological reality of the crush-depth they are preparing for. 
- **The 14-Day Clock vs Senne's Clock:** The text mentions both clocks, but Senne's deterioration needs to feel more immediate to justify diving before the remaining three coordinates are derived. Why are they preparing the hull now instead of running more tests?
- **Prael's Location:** If the Archive is stepping in via the 14-day clock, where is Prael now that Maret has defected? A brief mention of Prael's status would tie up the loose institutional thread.

### 3. Recommended Edits
- **Revise Scene 1 (The Hull) to add the crush-depth calculus:** Have Sable briefly review the theoretical pressure physics of the metallic hydrogen core. Frame it not as fear, but as data. She expects to be crushed. She is armor-plating the ship to buy seconds, not survival.
- **Revise Scene 3 (The Weld) to emphasize Senne's timeline:** Add a realization that they cannot wait to derive coordinates 10, 11, and 12 through safe tests. They have to dive *now* and derive the final coordinates during the descent, because Senne won't last another 48 hours.
